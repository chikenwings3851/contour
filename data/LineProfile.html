Sources & References

Ground Elevation — Ontario LiDAR DTM
0.5m-resolution digital terrain model, Land Information Ontario (LIO), Government of Ontario. Assembled from many separate regional LiDAR survey projects flown at different times (roughly 2014–2023 depending on location) — coverage and currency vary by area. View service ↗
Aerial Imagery — Ontario Orthophotography
SWOOP/COOP aerial photo programs, Land Information Ontario (LIO) — the same imagery used in the MaxDrainageMap tool. Vintage varies by region/county. View service ↗
Datum & Reference System
Vertical: CGVD2013 (Canadian Geodetic Vertical Datum of 2013, gravity-related height, metres) — from the DTM service's own published metadata. Horizontal: WGS84 (EPSG:4326); the DTM's native raster is stored in Web Mercator (EPSG:3857).
